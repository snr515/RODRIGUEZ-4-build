Who We Are

Ground Cafe is a cozy neighborhood coffee shop, beautiful plants, and meaningful moments.We believe a great cup of coffee can create a simple pause in a busy day.

Our space is designed to feel warm, welcoming, and relaxed. Whether you are meeting a friend, reading a book, working, or simply enjoying some quiet time, Grounded is a place where you can feel at home.

Come grab a cup, stay awhile, and get grounded.
